Founder & Visionary

FRAINCES XAVIER

Founder & Managing Director, KRUPA Packers and Movers
"When families and businesses trust us with their belongings, they are trusting us with their memories and livelihoods. Our mission at Krupa Packers and Movers is to deliver 100% safety, utmost care, and complete peace of mind with every move across India."

Frainces Xavier is the visionary Founder and Managing Director behind KRUPA Packers and Movers. Under his strategic direction and hands-on leadership, Krupa Packers and Movers has grown into one of India’s premier logistics and relocation service providers, trusted by thousands of families, corporate enterprises, and institutions.

Driven by a deep passion for customer satisfaction and operational excellence, Frainces established Krupa Packers on the core values of safety, transparency, punctuality, and personalized care. He pioneered multi-layer protective packaging standards, GPS-tracked container transport fleets, and 24/7 dedicated support teams to eliminate moving stress for clients nationwide.

With over 15+ years of industry experience, Frainces continues to personally oversee team training, quality benchmarks, and service innovation, ensuring every single move—whether local household shifting or complex inter-state corporate relocation—is executed with zero damage and maximum efficiency.
